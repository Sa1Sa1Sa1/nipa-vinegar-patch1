Welcome to the official website of the Nipa Vinegar Mosquito Patch — a natural, eco-friendly, and Filipino-made innovation designed to protect families from mosquitoes.

This is a Capstone Project/Product created by Grade 12 STEM students of Our Lady of La Salette, St. Mary's Academy of Hagonoy.
About the Nipa Tree

The Nipa palm (Nypa fruticans) is a unique mangrove species that grows in coastal wetlands, riverbanks, and brackish waters. It is highly valued in the Philippines for its ecological, cultural, and economic importance.

Ecological Benefits:
Stabilizes riverbanks, prevents erosion, improves water quality, and supports aquatic life.
Cultural Value:
Used for roofing, weaving, and vinegar production supporting local industries.
References / RRL: Aquino et al., 2021; Santos, 2019
How Nipa Vinegar Repels Mosquitoes

Nipa vinegar contains acetic acid and natural compounds that disrupt mosquito scent receptors.

Safe, wearable, chemical-free protection for the whole family.
