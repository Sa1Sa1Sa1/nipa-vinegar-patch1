Welcome!
Welcome to the official website of the Nipa Vinegar Mosquito Patch — a natural, eco-friendly, Filipino-made innovation designed to protect families from mosquitoes.

This is a Capstone Project by Grade 12 STEM students of St. Mary's Academy of Hagonoy.
About the Nipa Tree
The Nipa palm (Nypa fruticans) grows in coastal wetlands and mangroves. It supports biodiversity and provides livelihood through vinegar production.
How Nipa Vinegar Repels Mosquitoes
Nipa vinegar contains acetic acid that interferes with mosquito scent receptors, making treated areas unattractive to them.
Where Nipa Trees Can Be Found
Bulacan, Pampanga, Bataan, Visayas, and Mindanao coastal areas.
