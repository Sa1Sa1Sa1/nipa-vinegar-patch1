About the Nipa Tree

The Nipa palm (Nypa fruticans) is a unique mangrove species that grows in coastal wetlands, riverbanks, and brackish waters.

Ecological: Prevents erosion & supports biodiversity.
Cultural: Used for roofing, vinegar, and livelihoods.
RRL: Aquino et al. (2021); Santos (2019)
How It Repels Mosquitoes

Natural acids in nipa vinegar disrupt mosquito scent receptors.
