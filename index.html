Welcome!
Welcome to the official website of the Nipa Vinegar Mosquito Patch — a natural, eco-friendly Filipino innovation created by Grade 12 STEM students of St. Mary’s Academy of Hagonoy.
About the Nipa Tree

The Nipa palm (Nypa fruticans) grows in coastal wetlands and brackish waters across the Philippines.

Ecological Benefits
Stabilizes riverbanks, prevents erosion, and supports aquatic life.
Cultural Value
Used for roofing, weaving, and vinegar production.
References / RRL: Aquino et al. (2021); Santos (2019)
Vinegar & Mosquito Control

Nipa vinegar contains natural acids that disrupt mosquito scent receptors.

Why use a patch?

Safe for kids and adults
Eco-friendly & biodegradable
Convenient for activities
Supports local nipa growers
References / RRL: Villanueva (2020); Reyes et al. (2018)
Locations
Hagonoy, Paombong, Pampanga Delta, Bataan, Visayas, Mindanao
